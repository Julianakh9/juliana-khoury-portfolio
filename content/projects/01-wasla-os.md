Worked on product discovery, requirements, architecture, data modeling, and explainable matching concepts for a placement workflow that brings together candidate knowledge, job requirements, workflow state, and provenance.

The project emphasizes human-in-the-loop decision making, explainability, structured candidate and job memory, and reducing fragmented manual placement workflows.
