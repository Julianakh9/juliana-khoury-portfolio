The ALU supported ADD, SUB, AND, OR, XOR, and barrel-right shift operations, with a 4-bit Brent–Kung adder used in the arithmetic unit. The design included registers, multiplexers, decoding and control logic, and full-chip integration.

We performed DRC and LVS verification, extracted-layout analysis, and schematic vs. post-layout timing comparison. We also evaluated the critical path, maximum operating frequency, power, area, and performance across multiple supply voltages.
